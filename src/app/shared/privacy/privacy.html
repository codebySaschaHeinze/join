Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2025

1. General information

This Privacy Policy explains how we handle personal data when you use the student project “Join”. The project was developed as part of a web development bootcamp at the Developer Akademie GmbH and is used solely for educational purposes.

2. Data controller

The responsible persons (“we”) are the project team listed in the Legal Notice. For any privacy-related questions or to exercise your data protection rights, you can contact us at:

Email: kontakt@saschaheinze.de

3. Hosting and server log files (IONOS)

This project is hosted by IONOS SE, Elgendorfer Straße 57, 56410 Montabaur, Germany. When you access Join, certain technical information is automatically collected and stored in so-called server log files by our hosting provider. This may include:

  • IP address of the requesting device
  • Date and time of access
  • URL and requested file
  • Referrer URL (previously visited page)
  • Browser type and version, operating system, and device type

This data is processed exclusively for the purpose of ensuring the secure and reliable operation of the website, troubleshooting, and protecting against misuse or attacks (e.g. DDoS). The data is not combined with other data sources and is not used for marketing or profiling. The legal basis for this processing is our legitimate interest in the secure and efficient provision of our online offer (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

4. Purpose of data processing

We process personal data in order to provide and manage user accounts, enable secure login and authentication, and store the content you create within Join (such as tasks, contacts, and related project information). In addition, certain data is processed to ensure the security, stability, and proper technical functioning of the Service. We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or analytics.

5. Categories of data processed

  • Account data (Firebase Authentication): email address, password (stored only as a secure hash), account creation time, last login time, and authentication tokens required for secure sessions.
  • User-generated content (Join): all content you create within the application, such as tasks, contacts, assigned users, comments, and related project information.
  • Technical data (IONOS & Firebase): IP address, browser type and version, operating system, device information, referrer URL, date and time of access, and error logs. These data are necessary for security, troubleshooting, and ensuring the stable operation of the Service.

6. Legal bases

We process personal data on the following legal bases under the GDPR:

  • Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR – performance of a contract: required for the creation and management of user accounts, login and authentication, and the storage of user-generated content within Join.
  • Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR – legitimate interests: required to ensure the security, stability, and proper technical functioning of the website and Firebase systems (e.g., server log files, authentication logs, error logs, misuse prevention).

We do not rely on consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) because Join does not use analytics, advertising technologies, or non-essential cookies.

7. Firebase (Google) services

Join uses Firebase, a service provided by Google LLC, 1600 Amphitheatre Parkway, Mountain View, CA 94043, USA, and for users in the EEA operated by Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland. Firebase acts as our data processor under a Data Processing Agreement.

The following Firebase services are used:

  • Firebase Authentication: handles user registration, login, logout, password storage (securely hashed), session tokens, and account management.
  • Firestore / Realtime Database: stores user-generated content such as tasks, contacts, assignments, and timestamps created within Join.

Firebase stores data primarily on servers located in the EU region (e.g. europe-west). However, Google may process data in third countries (including the USA). In such cases, Google uses the EU Standard Contractual Clauses to ensure an adequate level of data protection.

Firebase Authentication may set technically necessary cookies or local storage entries to maintain secure login sessions. These are essential for the function of the Service and do not contain advertising or tracking identifiers.

8. Data sharing

Personal data is not shared with third parties unless required for the technical operation of Join. The following service providers are involved:

  • IONOS SE (hosting provider): processes technical server log files to ensure the secure and reliable delivery of the website.
  • Firebase (Google): processes account data and user-generated content on our behalf under a Data Processing Agreement.

No data is sold, rented, or disclosed for advertising, analytics, or marketing purposes.

9. Data retention and deletion

Personal data is stored only for as long as it is necessary for the operation of Join or as required by applicable law.

  • Account data (Firebase Authentication): Your account and authentication data remain stored until you delete your account or request deletion. Once your account is deleted, Firebase removes the associated authentication data immediately.
  • User-generated content (Firestore / Realtime Database): Tasks, contacts, assignments, and other project-related entries are stored as long as your account exists. After account deletion, this data is deleted or anonymised within a short period. Backups may continue to exist for a limited time due to technical necessities.
  • Technical logs (IONOS & Firebase): Server log files and error logs are retained only for a short duration (typically a few days to a few weeks) to ensure security, troubleshoot issues, and prevent misuse. These logs are not used for analytics or profiling.

You may request the deletion of your data at any time via the contact email provided in this Privacy Policy.

10. Cookies and tracking

Join does not use analytics tools, tracking technologies, or non-essential cookies. No profiling or advertising technologies are used.

Firebase Authentication uses technically necessary cookies or browser storage entries to maintain secure login sessions. These cookies are essential for the functionality of the Service and do not contain advertising identifiers or tracking information. They are stored only for the duration of your authenticated session.

Apart from these essential authentication cookies, no other cookies are set by the Service.

11. Your rights under the GDPR

As a data subject, you have the following rights regarding the processing of your personal data:

  • Right of access (Art. 15 GDPR)
  • Right to rectification (Art. 16 GDPR)
  • Right to erasure (Art. 17 GDPR)
  • Right to restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR)
  • Right to data portability (Art. 20 GDPR)
  • Right to object (Art. 21 GDPR)
  • Right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR)

To exercise any of these rights or for privacy-related concerns, please contact us via the email address provided in section 2. Data controller.

12. Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data and to prevent unauthorised access, loss, or misuse. User authentication is handled by Firebase Authentication, where passwords are never stored in plain text but managed using secure hashing mechanisms.

Data transmission to Join generally takes place over encrypted connections (HTTPS). However, no system or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect technical, legal, or operational changes. The latest version is always available within the Join project. Continued use of the Service after updates indicates acceptance of the revised Policy.
